# Futuro Pulse — trial access briefing

*Please read before you sign in. Reply to confirm you've read it.*

**What this is.** Futuro Pulse is an internal dashboard we're trialling. It pulls live operational data about our centres from OWNA, LineLeader and Employment Hero — occupancy, enrolments, wages, incidents, people metrics — into one place, with an AI assistant that can answer questions about it.

**What you'll see.** Real numbers about real centres. Trial logins see **aggregates only** — no children's, families' or staff names. If you ever see a named child, family or staff member, that is a fault: tell us straight away.

**What we ask of you**

1. **Use it for your role only.** Look at what you need to do your job; don't browse for the sake of it.
2. **No screenshots or sharing outside Futuro.** Not in group chats, not to families, not to other services.
3. **Don't type names into the AI box.** Your question is sent to Anthropic, an external AI provider to be answered. Ask "how is occupancy tracking at Austral", not about a specific child or colleague.
4. **Don't share your login.** One person, one login. Log out on shared devices.
5. **Tell us what's wrong.** Use *Share feedback* in the sidebar for anything confusing, missing or incorrect — that's the whole point of the trial.
6. **Report anything you think you shouldn't be able to see** to your centre director or the Privacy Officer the same day.

**Privacy in one paragraph.** The dashboard holds personal information about children, families and staff that Futuro already holds in its source systems. It's protected by login and access roles. It is currently hosted on Render; Australian hosting has not been established, and we're formalising its privacy arrangements as part of this trial. Feedback you submit is attached to your name and read only by administrators. Questions: contact the Privacy Officer.
